# SamkhyaAcademy Visual Master Contract

This contract makes the approved UX references the visual source of truth for every public, learner, Venture Builder, organization, CMS and operations screen. It does not authorize copying obsolete claims or SAP positioning; content remains governed by the latest BRD.

## Governing masters

1. `02_Courses_and_Learning/02_AI_Academy_Page.png` governs the overall platform composition: integrated photographic hero, editorial section rhythm, navy feature panels, proof bands, conversion bands and footer.
2. `02_Courses_and_Learning/04_Video_Lesson.png` governs every authenticated learning experience: curriculum rail, dominant lesson workspace, contextual right rail, progress, resources, next action, notes and discussion.
3. `03_Entrepreneurship/01_Venture_Builder_Landing.png` governs program storytelling and Venture Builder: photographic narrative, horizontal stage visualization, people/mentor proof, outcomes, cohort facts and strong conversion closure.

The remaining approved references specialize these three masters for Full Stack, Venture workflow, organization and CRM contexts.

## Non-negotiable visual rules

- Poppins is the product typeface. Product text must never be artificially reduced to mimic a downscaled screenshot.
- Desktop body text is normally 12–15px; persistent navigation and dense metadata may use 10–11px; nothing essential may render below 10px.
- Primary headings use deep navy and 700–800 weight. Indigo is reserved for emphasis and action, not large undifferentiated backgrounds.
- Use `#061b43`/`#0b214c` navy, `#4438df` indigo, `#24cfa4` green-mint, clean white, soft neutral surfaces and restrained gold accents.
- Use the reusable SVG Samkhya mark and Lucide line icons. Unicode symbols and arbitrary letter-based pseudo-icons are prohibited in final screens.
- Dashboard iconography is functional: navigation, KPI cards, search, filter, sort, columns, export, section headings, row actions, approval and publishing controls each use a consistent line icon with a visible text label and accessible name.
- Icons must improve scanning and hierarchy without becoming ornament. Reuse the same icon for the same action across learner, organization, Venture, CMS and operations dashboards.
- Hero imagery must be integrated with gradients and content, never placed as a disconnected rectangular afterthought.
- Cards use thin neutral borders, 8–14px radii and purposeful grouping. Avoid generic four-card KPI grids unless the reference context requires them.
- A 1440px review screen should use at least 85% of its available experience canvas for meaningful content. Unexplained blank bands larger than 96px fail visual QA.
- Empty real estate is a hard rejection condition. Every visible region must contain purposeful learning, decision, navigation, evidence or conversion content; desktop pages may not use oversized empty cards, artificial full-page height or sparse canvases as decoration.
- Application UX states are reviewed at the customer viewport represented by the master. Full-page capture is used only when the page itself contains continuous meaningful content below the fold.
- Learner workspaces keep curriculum, content and context visible together. Continuation states appear as drawers, modals, inline results or changed workspaces—not separate empty pages.
- Every interactive journey needs at least its default, active/in-progress, validation/error, success and next-step states where applicable.

## Acceptance

Screen count is inventory only, never evidence of quality. A screen is accepted only after reference comparison at its native review viewport, readable typography inspection, icon audit, overflow check, content-density check and connected-journey verification.
