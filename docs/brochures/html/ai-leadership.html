Detailed module curriculum · 1-2

Build the foundations.

The module names, lesson sequence and evidence below match the course learning workspace.

1AI Reality for Leaders

Evaluate AI proposals by capability, evidence and consequences of error.

  1. 1.1LLM capability and limitationsFluent output can conceal missing evidence.
  2. 1.2RAG, fine-tuning and SLMsRetrieval supplies external context; fine-tuning changes learned behavior; a smaller model may suit a bounded task.
  3. 1.3Agents and autonomous workflowsAutonomy changes who selects the next step and therefore changes risk.
  4. 1.4Voice, vision and multimodal opportunitiesVoice, image and document workflows can expand access but also introduce recognition errors and privacy concerns.

You will produce: Capability assessment and decision-boundary map.

2Prioritize and Fund

Choose an opportunity that the organization can responsibly validate.

  1. 2.1Opportunity discoveryStart with an observed bottleneck and a named user group.
  2. 2.2Data and permission readinessData readiness includes quality, access rights, provenance and ongoing maintenance.
  3. 2.3Build vs buy vs partnerCompare build, buy and partner options against integration, control, skills, cost and exit needs.
  4. 2.4AI economics and ROIEstimate value from a measured baseline and explicit assumptions.

You will produce: Opportunity portfolio and assumption-based business case.

3Govern and Operate

Assign responsibility for safe operation and meaningful adoption.

  1. 3.1Risk and human controlIdentify the consequences of error and place human control where it can prevent harm.
  2. 3.2Security and auditabilityAuditability requires knowing who requested, approved and executed an action.
  3. 3.3Operating modelAssign business ownership, technical operation, incident response and change approval.
  4. 3.4Adoption and changeAdoption requires role-specific training and feedback channels.

You will produce: Governance charter, access matrix and adoption plan.

490-Day Execution Blueprint

Present a 90-day plan with a decision gate rather than a guaranteed outcome.

  1. 4.1Select priority use casesSelect a small set of use cases that share a measurable goal and a ready owner.
  2. 4.2Define pilot and metricsDefine the pilot’s audience, baseline, measures and guardrails before launch.
  3. 4.3Set governance checkpointsSchedule checkpoints for data access, evaluation, user readiness and incident handling.
  4. 4.4Present the 90-day planPresent scope, owners, cost assumptions, delivery phases and stop/scale criteria.

You will produce: 90-day execution blueprint and executive review deck.

Inside module 1: a worked example

Proposal review: “AI resolves all service tickets.”
Clarify: which ticket classes, source evidence, permitted actions and escalation?
Require: examples of unsupported questions and a denied change
Decision: bounded assisted workflow before broader autonomy
