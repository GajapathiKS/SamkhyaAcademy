# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Module 7: Scale and Production

Prepare a responsible operating plan and present the evidence behind the venture.

### 7.1 Operating model

An operating model assigns decisions, delivery work, support and escalation. Identify bottlenecks that depend on one founder. Document repeatable work before hiring or automating so the team knows what capability it is trying to scale.

### 7.2 Security and compliance readiness

Identify sensitive data, access boundaries and obligations that require qualified advice. Build an issue register with owners and due dates. This lesson is a readiness exercise, not legal advice or a declaration that the venture is compliant.

### 7.3 Scale plan

A scale plan connects demand assumptions with capacity, cost and quality. Model a plausible increase and identify which limit is reached first. Define checkpoints and rollback options instead of treating growth as an irreversible all-at-once launch.

### 7.4 Final showcase

The final showcase presents the problem, evidence, model, prototype, pilot results and remaining risks. Separate facts from forecasts. Course certification reflects the stated learning requirements; investor introductions, venture selection and funding decisions remain independent.

## Worked lab

Scale scenario: 100 -> 500 active customers
Check: support hours, service cost, data permissions and failure recovery
Constraint: one reviewer can assess 40 cases/day
Decision: improve workflow and test capacity before widening acquisition
Showcase appendix: evidence links and unresolved assumptions.

A scale plan is credible when it identifies constraints, not when it assumes every cost and process scales linearly.
