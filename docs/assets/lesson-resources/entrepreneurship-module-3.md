# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Module 3: Business Model

Translate a validated problem into a testable business model.

### 3.1 Value proposition

A value proposition connects a customer job to a specific improvement over the current alternative. State the benefit in the customer’s terms. A feature such as an AI dashboard matters only if it changes a useful decision or outcome.

### 3.2 Revenue and pricing

Separate how value is delivered from how revenue is collected. A subscription, usage charge or service fee changes incentives and cash timing. Treat a proposed price as a hypothesis and test it ethically rather than presenting it as established willingness to pay.

### 3.3 Unit economics

Unit economics estimates contribution at a meaningful unit such as a customer or transaction. Keep variable costs, fixed costs and acquisition cost distinct. Explain the period and assumptions; an attractive gross margin does not guarantee positive cash flow.

### 3.4 Business model review

A review tests whether the customer, channel, revenue and cost assumptions fit together. Identify the assumption most likely to invalidate the model. Make a revision based on evidence instead of adding unsupported market-size or revenue claims.
