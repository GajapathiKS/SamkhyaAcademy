# Forward Deployed AI Leadership

## Module 2: Prioritize and Fund

Choose an opportunity that the organization can responsibly validate.

### 2.1 Opportunity discovery

Start with an observed bottleneck and a named user group. Record the present process, exceptions and baseline. An opportunity is stronger when an owner can explain the decision it will improve, not merely the technology they want to purchase.

### 2.2 Data and permission readiness

Data readiness includes quality, access rights, provenance and ongoing maintenance. A large document collection is not automatically usable. Establish who owns permission changes and how stale or disputed information is handled.

### 2.3 Build vs buy vs partner

Compare build, buy and partner options against integration, control, skills, cost and exit needs. Identify dependencies and switching costs. A vendor feature list does not prove that the organization can operate the resulting workflow.

### 2.4 AI economics and ROI

Estimate value from a measured baseline and explicit assumptions. Include review time, integration, support and error costs, not only model usage. Report a range and sensitivity analysis; teaching examples are not financial forecasts or investment advice.

## Worked lab

Candidate scorecard: value | feasibility | data readiness | consequence of error
Pilot cost: integration + user training + review + operation
Benefit hypothesis: reduced handling time without increased consequential errors
Decision owner: business lead with risk and technical input

A scorecard supports discussion; the weights and assumptions must remain visible rather than creating false precision.
