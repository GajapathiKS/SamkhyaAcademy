# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Turn an idea into a falsifiable customer hypothesis

A founder proposes crop-advisory software for small farmers. Before building the product, the team needs evidence that a specific problem is important enough to change behavior.

Separate the customer segment, observed problem and proposed solution. Interviews should explore recent behavior rather than ask people to praise an idea. Record evidence that contradicts the hypothesis as well as evidence that supports it. A mentor gate checks the quality of the evidence and the next experiment; it does not guarantee commercial success or funding.

## Worked example

Hypothesis: a defined farmer segment struggles with a specific decision.
Interview: ask about the most recent occurrence and current workaround.
Evidence: record context, frequency, consequence and direct observations.
Synthesis: group patterns and note contradictions.
Next test: choose one uncertain assumption and an observable outcome.

The stage artifact is an evidence-backed problem statement, not a feature list. The team may narrow the segment, revise the problem or stop the idea based on what it learns. Consent and privacy govern how interview notes are stored and shared.
