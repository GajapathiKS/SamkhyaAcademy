# Forward Deployed AI Leadership

## Module 3: Govern and Operate

Assign responsibility for safe operation and meaningful adoption.

### 3.1 Risk and human control

Identify the consequences of error and place human control where it can prevent harm. A reviewer needs time, evidence and authority, not just a button to approve. Define stop conditions before the pilot begins.

### 3.2 Security and auditability

Auditability requires knowing who requested, approved and executed an action. Limit access and retain only necessary evidence. Confirm that logs do not expose sensitive content more broadly than the underlying system.

### 3.3 Operating model

Assign business ownership, technical operation, incident response and change approval. Define escalation paths and handover responsibilities. An AI project without an operating owner can succeed in a demo and fail immediately after launch.

### 3.4 Adoption and change

Adoption requires role-specific training and feedback channels. Explain what the system cannot do and how to challenge an answer. Track useful task completion, not only logins, and avoid treating reluctance as a problem to solve by removing human oversight.

## Worked lab

Control record: action | risk | approver | evidence | stop condition
Example: invoice recommendation -> human approval before payment
Incident owner: operations lead
Change owner: product lead with risk review
Training: recognizing unsupported or uncertain output

The operating model makes responsibility visible when an exception occurs, not only during normal use.
