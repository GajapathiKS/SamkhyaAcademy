# Forward Deployed AI Leadership

## Choose a pilot with a measurable decision gate

A leadership team is comparing an internal knowledge assistant, automated sales outreach and invoice triage. It can fund one small pilot before making a larger commitment.

Start with a workflow baseline and a named accountable owner. Compare value, feasibility, data readiness and consequence of error. A high-volume task is not automatically the best first pilot if permissions are unclear or errors are costly. Define the decision to be made at the end of the pilot before buying tools or expanding access.

## Worked example

Pilot: invoice triage, with human approval
Baseline: record current handling time and correction rate
Scope: one document type and one operational team
Controls: approved data, access limits, escalation owner
Decision gate: compare measured results with agreed thresholds
Outcomes: stop, revise scope, or approve controlled expansion

The pilot charter turns an AI idea into a governed experiment. Its success thresholds are agreed by the business and risk owners rather than invented in the course material. A 90-day plan includes discovery, a bounded pilot and an evidence-based scale decision.
