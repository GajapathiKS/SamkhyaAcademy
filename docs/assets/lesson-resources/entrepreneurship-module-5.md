# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Module 5: Pilot Launch

Plan a limited pilot with a clear audience, support boundary and rollback decision.

### 5.1 Pilot design

A pilot has a limited scope, duration, participant set and purpose. Explain what participants will receive and what remains experimental. Set a stop condition for harmful or misleading behavior rather than waiting for a final presentation.

### 5.2 Go-to-market experiment

A go-to-market experiment tests one channel and one message with a defined audience. Track the full path from exposure to qualified interest. Clicks alone do not demonstrate a useful acquisition channel.

### 5.3 Launch checklist

A launch checklist covers access, support, privacy, incident handling, ownership and data recovery. Assign a responsible person to each item. An unchecked critical item should change the launch decision, not disappear into a completion percentage.

### 5.4 Launch gate

A launch gate compares evidence with pre-agreed readiness criteria. Record approve, revise or stop and the reasons. In the course, this is a simulated review; publishing a lesson or completing a quiz does not authorize a real venture launch.

## Worked lab

Pilot charter: 10 consenting participants; 2 weeks; synthetic recommendations
Measure: task completion and misunderstanding reports
Support owner: named team member
Stop: participant treats simulated output as operational advice
Rollback: disable recommendation screen; preserve consented research notes.

The stop condition is part of the pilot design, not evidence that the team expects the experiment to fail.
