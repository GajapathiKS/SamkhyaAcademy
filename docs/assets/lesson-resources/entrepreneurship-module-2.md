# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Module 2: Customer Validation

Plan discovery interviews that can disprove your hypothesis as well as support it.

### 2.1 Interview planning

Prepare open questions about recent behavior and ask for a concrete example. Seek consent before recording. Decide how notes will be stored and anonymized. A script should guide inquiry, not pressure an interviewee into endorsing the idea.

### 2.2 Customer discovery

Ask what happened the last time the problem occurred, what the person tried and what it cost in time or effort. Avoid pitching your solution first. Hypothetical enthusiasm is weaker evidence than actual behavior and current workarounds.

### 2.3 Validation evidence

Synthesize notes by segment, situation and recurring pattern. Preserve contradictory observations instead of deleting them as outliers. Distinguish an interview quote from your interpretation and record what additional evidence would change your conclusion.

### 2.4 Mentor validation gate

Review whether the evidence supports continuing, narrowing or revising the problem. The course exercise uses a documented decision with uncertainty. An actual venture validation gate is a separate review of a submitted application and artifacts, not an automatic course-completion event.

## Worked lab

Interview prompt: “Tell me about the last irrigation decision you found difficult.”
Follow-up: “What did you do next?”
Evidence table: situation | workaround | consequence | quote | interpretation
Decision: narrow the segment if problems differ materially between groups.

The question elicits a past event rather than an optimistic forecast about a product that does not exist.
