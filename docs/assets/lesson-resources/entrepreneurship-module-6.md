# Entrepreneurship & Venture Builder

## Module 6: Traction and Learning

Use evidence to decide what to improve, continue or stop.

### 6.1 Metrics

Choose a metric that represents the behavior the venture needs, not merely activity. State its numerator, denominator, time window and exclusions. Distinguish acquisition, activation, retention and revenue rather than combining them into an ambiguous growth score.

### 6.2 Feedback loops

A feedback loop connects observation to a prioritized change and a follow-up measurement. Tag feedback by segment and context. Do not let one loud request silently become the product roadmap without checking whether it represents the intended audience.

### 6.3 Experiment results

Report experiment results against the original question and acknowledge sample limitations. Separate observed differences from causal claims. If several variables changed together, explain why the result cannot identify which change caused the effect.

### 6.4 Traction review

A traction review combines quantitative behavior, customer evidence and operating costs. Record what improved and what did not. A mentor’s positive review is not proof of product-market fit or a guarantee of investment.
